# The Neon Playbook

> Four-act workshop musical inside abandoned Studio 404: cobbled devices brought to life by imagination and old variety-show VHS reruns graduate from Neon's crooked 4-note starter hook through duets, a 3-voice canon, and backstage slapstick to a 7-voice polyphonic 'Glow' finale.

**Style & Continuity Rules:** Pure Studio 404 Found-Object Canon: wool felt, scuffed chassis, warm Fresnel cones (#16130f dark stage), zero digital buzzwords. EffectCraft rigs use foot-anchored Transform tweens, 3-pin Puppet deform, and rest/sing mouth swaps.
