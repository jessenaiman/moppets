# Cue and Neon on the cord mark

The live and Remotion stages select the action and exposure in `src/stage/RehearsalActor.tsx`. Each two-second EffectCraft action has 48 exposures and returns to a common rest pose. Cue's project is one directory up in `pinned-performance.ecproj`. Neon's is in `moppets/neon/pinned-performance.ecproj`. The heel and toe pins remain on their registration points while the pelvis, elbows, wrists, brows, scarf and coil carry the movement.

The two AniCraft cord projects draw the upstage and downstage arcs on opposite sides of the cast layer. The photographed plug and prompt scroll are from `storyboard/neon-playbook/flow-outputs/cue__cutouts_02.png`. Their key and spill suppression are editable in `moppets/cue/flow-ingredients.ecproj`. The newer Studio 404 Animation Bench sheets were not exported and are not body art in this build. Tabby's “Take a bow” uses the side-view plate and her own 18-pin bow in `moppets/tabby/pinned-performance.ecproj`.
