# One More Thing

The near wing searches the drawer. On “spinning top” the far wing winds back, flicks, then holds while the button top drops to the work surface. Chip bows before he waves.

Open `chip.ecproj` in EffectCraft. The registered cut-outs are in `parts/`. The two stage-action compositions share the same 48 body exposures, with independently selectable mouth states. Wing nulls and distal wing pins, neck pivot, PCB crest, tail fan.

Open `prop.anicraft` for the sixteen prop exposures. The live and video stages read the published atlases from `public/rehearsal/rigs/chip/solo/`.
