# Stage-right reactions

Tracking cues the tape, mimes ejecting it, then gestures to Hertz. The VHS cartridge travels out and back before the next exchange.

Open `tracking.ecproj` in EffectCraft. The registered cut-outs are in `parts/`. The two stage-action compositions share the same 48 body exposures, with independently selectable mouth states. Chassis pivot, both shoulder pivots, distal arm pins and VFD glow.

Open `prop.anicraft` for the sixteen prop exposures. The live and video stages read the published atlases from `public/rehearsal/rigs/tracking/solo/`.
