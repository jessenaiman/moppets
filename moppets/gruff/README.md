# Gruff — Close-Up

Gruff plays a four-string bass built from a radio drawer. The instrument hangs from the torso control; both cable arms are drawn in front of it. His pickup hand plucks on the finger-bass note attacks, and the fretting hand shifts along the neck with the pitch. The 76 BPM arrangement uses a long root and a short answering fifth, leaving the last root to ring.

Open `gruff.ecproj` for the collected body, two cable arms, boots, valve glow, head artwork and instrument. The two Close-Up character compositions share the same arm and instrument performance. The open version uses the front panel with an aperture behind the copper smile. The live player follows the ensemble clock and selects a mouth using the current jaw and lip telemetry.

The torso mesh has a cable-loop leader and two wire-nut followers delayed by 0.16 and 0.32 seconds. The head, torso and shoulder controls stage the verse and chorus. In the solo-film composition the opacity track on the open take comes from this recording’s jaw telemetry.

`gruff.anicraft` has a separate cable-stride study and a four-view turnaround sourced from the existing Gruff sheet. The individual solo plays from the Solos row in the app. The standalone render is `out/gruff-solo/close-up-bass.mp4`.
