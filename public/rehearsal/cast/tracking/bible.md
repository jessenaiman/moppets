# Tracking · Archive coach

Silver VCR, tape-slot mouth, button eyes and blinking clock. A single AV lead connects to Hertz.

![Current staging reference](staged-front.webp)

## Story

Tracking was the playback deck at the edit cart. An archive cassette was still inside at load-out, so the crew left him connected for the caretaker. Tracking takes the pencilled label, “for when they’re ready,” as an instruction to wait for the cast. Hertz lent him the AV lead between them. He wants to find the exact frame that teaches the cast what to do.

In **study hall**, Tracking stops and rewinds the good bit and looks to Hertz for the verdict. Use the slot flap for the offer and the blinking clock for the pause. The two coach together and dance as a pair.

## Acting

Tape-slot flap · Clock digits

The flap starts to offer a tape, hesitates, then takes it back. The clock blinks once in the silence.

1. Square up with the tape
2. Pause at the slot
3. Turn to Hertz for the answer

### The Good Bit

Rewind the dance tape when Tracking realises his mark was early. Let the cassette pause at the slot before it slides back. Listen while Hertz holds the picture, then catch the heel lifting before the toe. On the count, move with Hertz as a pair and leave enough slack in their single AV lead for the last shared step.
