## One Quiet Bar

Amplifier spent its first years under the rehearsal-room piano, where players checked leads without turning the room speakers on. On load-out day the piano movers shifted the piano. They left the little amp where it had always stood, beside a blue guitar from a school show. The small valve light was the only way to tell someone was still plugged in, and Amplifier still checks it before declaring a take over.

In **the held chord**, Amplifier sings “My chord is still a little warm” (beats 28–36). On “warm” at beat 32 the fret hand stops and the left valve holds amber. Keep the held atlas cell through beat 36, then use the cooling cell as Clacker points out that the light outlasted the sound.
