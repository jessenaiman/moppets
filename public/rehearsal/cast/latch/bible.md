# Latch · Talkback pedal

An old talkback footswitch with a rubber tread and a rear hinge that stays bolted to the floor.

![Current staging reference](staged-front.png)

## Story

Latch opened the line from the talkback desk to the floor. On the last load-out someone unscrewed the desk, but the pedal was still screwed down under it. Latch thinks the desk went for a walk. Gruff lent Latch a felt washer from his bass case to quiet the hinge through a ballad.

Latch listens for the thing somebody nearly said and writes songs from it. Latch wants everyone to get a turn, then fills the silence before they can take it. Cue calls places. Latch waits for the call even when everybody is already in place.

In **After You**, Cue announces a surprise and Clank doubles Latch in harmony. Latch cuts off “A sur—” by releasing the tread, waits through four silent beats and finally gives Cue room to say the desk came back.

## Acting

Rubber tread · Rear hinge

Lower the toe to open the line. Raise it a fraction and hold while listening. Put the click on release, even when it cuts a word short. Keep the rear hinge bolted to the floor.
