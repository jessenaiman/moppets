# Clacker · Keys player

Beige keyboard and synth-module body, two LED keycap eyes, small chest keyboard, green felt sleeves and brown boots. Keep hands on the keyboard plane.

## Acting

Wrists over the keys · Keycap eyes

The wrists hover, land on the chord, and release. The two LED eyes glance up only after the chord rings.

1. Hover over the keys
2. Play the chord
3. Lift the wrists as it rings
