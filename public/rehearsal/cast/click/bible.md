# Click · Percussion

A battery-powered quartz metronome in a dark red percussion apron. Rotary tempo dial, small amber tempo light, built-in speaker, side power switch and battery compartment.

![Current staging reference](staged-front.webp)

## Acting

Amber tempo lamp · Mallet hands

Count with the lamp, then let one hand steal the focus for an accented strike.

1. Flash the four-count
2. Strike the film can
3. Lift the sticks for the cutoff

## Model reference

Tabby stands 100 drawing units tall. Click is staged at 82 DU tall, with a projected front span of about 59.9 DU in this pose. Depth is estimated from supporting views.
