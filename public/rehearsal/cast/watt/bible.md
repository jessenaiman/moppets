# Watt · Power singer

Upright six-socket power strip, glowing red rocker, grounded plug and a bushy tail of rainbow leads. Keep the socket spacing fixed.

![Current staging reference](staged-front.webp)

## Acting

Red rocker · Cable tail

Tip the rocker on the stressed syllable. On release let one ripple travel to the tail tip while the six sockets hold still.

1. Lean toward the socket
2. Light the rocker on the accent
3. Settle the cable tail

## Model reference

Tabby stands 100 drawing units tall. Watt is staged at 95 DU tall, with a projected front span of about 62.4 DU in this pose. Depth is estimated from supporting views.

Keep every tail, limb, ear, plug lead and moving costume part attached at its own socket for the full take, including when held or briefly hidden. Hands can guide an attached tail. Any removal is a separately named comedy gag with its own explicit performance brief.
