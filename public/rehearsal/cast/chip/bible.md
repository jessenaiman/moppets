# Chip · Circuit bird

Floppy-disk torso, PCB feather crest, circuit wings and two small grounded bird feet. Keep the board silhouettes readable.

## Acting

Circuit-board crest · One wing

The crest cocks toward the question. One wing opens at the high note, holds, then folds against the floppy-disk body.

1. Cock the crest toward the sound
2. Open one wing on the high note
3. Fold the wing and land
