# Zap · Director

Upright early-1990s universal remote, small green LCD face, crowded rubber keypad, felt mitts and a sprung battery door. The mitt lands on a physical button.

![Current staging reference](staged-front.webp)

## Acting

Button-pressing mitt · LCD eyes · Battery door

The mitt hovers over the wrong button and commits. The eyes check the audience. Reserve one battery-door pop for the biggest miscue.

1. Inspect the keypad
2. Press a button with one mitt
3. Wave the next performer in

## Model reference

Tabby stands 100 drawing units tall. Zap is staged at 90 DU tall, with a projected front span of about 78.2 DU in this pose. Depth is estimated from supporting views.

Keep every tail, limb, ear, plug lead and moving costume part attached at its own socket for the full take, including when held or briefly hidden. Hands can guide an attached tail. Any removal is a separately named comedy gag with its own explicit performance brief.
