# Studio 404 house band

[Approved band photograph](../../public/rehearsal/band/house-band.webp)

They learned rock by sneaking back to the VCR after rehearsal. They know when to leave a hole in a riff, when to let a note ring, and when everyone should watch the bass player's hand. The ON AIR light has yet to come on. They play to the empty chairs as though this might be the take that starts their show.

| Performer | Found electric device | Musical job | Where to look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gruff | A single tangled length of broadcast multicore cable | Upright bass made from a metal tub, boom pole and one string. One attached lead connects him to the rest of the band. | The huge plucking hand pulls sideways; his coil mouth smiles into the gap. |
| Amplifier | A valve amplifier wearing its plum dust cover | Plays the studio’s scuffed blue six-string electric guitar. “Amplifier” is the working cast label. | Follow the fret hand for the figure, then one warming valve on the held chord. |
| Click | A battery-powered quartz metronome with a speaker and amber timing lamp | Counts in and plays the film-can drums and reel cymbal. | The lamp owns the count; the mallets take the accent. |
| Thrum | A powered broadcast patch and level-monitor module | Operates the little tape-echo return desk. | One hand opens the return, then the paired VU needles decay. |
| Clacker | A studio synthesizer | Plays the keys across his chest. | The wrists land on the offbeat, then the keycap eyes glance up. |

The first staging pose separates arms, sleeves and held props against a clear backing. For ordinary motion all limbs, tails, ear mounts, costume pieces and attached leads remain joined at their sockets throughout. An intentional detachment gag gets its own directed take. The four reusable videos and their decoded frame ranges belong under `reusable_takes/`.

The production lineup is the five performers in the approved picture. [Earlier workshop studies](../../storyboard/house-band-workshop/README.md) are kept separately.
