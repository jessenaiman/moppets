# Brief: Zap, the universal remote (two images)

Make **two images with your image generation tool and nothing else**. Don't run any command, don't
edit or process images. Save them as `out/moppet-art/zap/rest.png` and `out/moppet-art/zap/sing.png`
(create the folder), then list which is which. `out/` is gitignored: commit nothing.

Read `docs/briefs/moppet-world.md` first: Zap is in the roster and in "The AV cart and study hall".

## Every image

> A handmade felt puppet character photographed like a stop-motion film set: soft studio light,
> visible stitching, wool felt and worn fabric texture, real vintage electronics with scuffs, patina
> and small glowing lights. Warm, kind, expressive; never angry or sneering. Original design, not
> resembling any existing puppet franchise. One character, full body, three-quarter front view,
> centred, with space around it and its feet near the bottom of the frame. Flat solid chroma-blue
> background (#0047BB), even lighting, no shadow on the background. Square image. No text, no logos.

## Zap

> An early-1990s universal TV/VCR remote control standing upright as the character's whole body:
> chunky charcoal-black plastic, slightly scuffed, far too many rubber buttons in grey, red, green
> and yellow down its front (number pad, a big round directional pad, tiny unlabelled buttons).
> At the top, a small rectangular green-backlit LCD screen is its face, showing two simple pixel
> eyes and a pixel mouth. Above the screen, a dark red infrared window like a forehead. A battery
> door on its back, held shut with a strip of tape, with one bright AA battery (borrowed from a
> power strip friend) peeking out of the side. Short felt arms in mustard corduroy with four-fingered
> oatmeal felt hands, one hand pressing one of its own buttons; short felt legs in grey felt boots.
> Eager, overconfident, a little nervous, as if it has just appointed itself the director.

Make **two images that match exactly** (same pose, size, framing and lighting): first **"rest"**
with the pixel mouth closed in a small confident smile, then **"sing"** with the pixel mouth open
in a round "O" as if singing a bright note. Only the pixels of the mouth change.

Check both before saving: square, one whole character, flat chroma-blue background, matching pose.
Redo any that fail.
