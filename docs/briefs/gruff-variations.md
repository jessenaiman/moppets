# Brief: four design variations of Gruff, the wire Moppet

Make **four images with your image generation tool and nothing else**. Don't run any command, don't
edit or process the images, don't write files: generate the four and, at the end, list which image
is which variation. They're design explorations, not final art.

## Who Gruff is

The Moppets are devices left behind in an old TV studio that is now a props warehouse. They
assembled themselves, clumsily, and helped each other: every Moppet carries a part borrowed from
another. Gruff sings: "I was a box of tubes half dead". He is an old valve (vacuum-tube) amplifier
that became a big, gentle bass-singing creature. He looks gruff and sounds gravelly, but he is soft
inside: sleepy, slow to speak, careful with small things. Never angry or sneering; at his
grumpiest, a fond theatrical grumble.

## What makes him a Moppet

- The amplifier **is** his body: a battered wooden-and-metal cabinet with a cloth speaker grille for
  a big round belly and a row of glass vacuum tubes along his top or chest that glow warm orange.
  Some tubes are dark (half dead); the lit ones glow brighter when he's happy and flicker when he's
  unsure.
- His fur is **wires**: a big shaggy mane and coat of tangled cables in muted purples, mauves and
  greys (old cloth-covered cords, coiled phone cords, speaker wire), loose and springy so it can
  sway and bounce when animated. Two old plugs stick up as horns. A frayed cord for a tail, its plug
  dangling.
- A gentle face: sleepy, kind eyes (two round dials or two warm indicator lamps) peeking out from
  the wire mane, a wide soft felt mouth that can open.
- Felt does the rest: big soft felt hands and feet, visible stitching.
- One borrowed part: a felt patch with a rainbow cable stitch (Tabby's) on one arm.
- Original design, not resembling any existing puppet franchise.

## Style for every image

> A handmade felt puppet character photographed like a stop-motion film set: soft studio light,
> visible stitching, wool felt and worn fabric texture, real vintage electronics with scuffs, patina
> and warm glowing valves. Full body, three-quarter front view, standing, on a flat solid
> chroma-blue background (#0047BB), even lighting. No text, no logos.

## The four variations

1. **Big and cuddly.** Wide, round and tall, more mane than body: the wire coat falls almost to his
   feet, the amplifier belly peeks through, the glowing tubes like a crown on his head.
2. **Amp head.** The amplifier is his head and face (the grille is his mouth area, the tubes a row
   of glowing teeth-like lights above, two dials for eyes), wires spilling down as his mane and
   body, felt limbs.
3. **Upright and dapper.** Slimmer, standing tall like a bass singer about to sing, the wires combed
   and tied back neatly with a tape strip, a little proud, holding one felt hand to his chest.
4. **Bedraggled and sweet.** Half his tubes dark, wires frizzy and tangled, one plug-horn bent, but a
   shy, warm smile and one tube glowing bright: the "half dead" box that is very much alive.
