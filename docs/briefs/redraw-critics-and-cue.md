## Tracking and Degauss: one image of the pair, three versions

Best friends and opinionated film critics who own the studio's archive tapes. They need each other
to watch anything: Tracking (the VCR) plays tapes but has no screen; Degauss (the CRT television) has
the screen but nothing to play. **An old AV cable runs from the back of Tracking to the side of
Degauss**, clearly visible, a gentle loop of cable between them, like holding hands.

Both **standing** side by side, mid-dance together: Tracking does a small two-step with one felt foot
lifted; Degauss sways with one arm raised in a flourish. **No seats, no velvet, no floor.** Tracking
is fussy but lovable: bushy grey eyebrows **raised in amusement, never furrowed, frowning or cross**,
a small wry smile. Degauss is grand and kindly, a glowing smile on his screen. Keep Tracking's tweed
scarf and blinking 12:00, Degauss's aerial, dials and corduroy.

Make three images of exactly the same pose, framing and lighting; only the mouths differ:

1. **critics-rest**: both mouths closed, both smiling.
2. **critics-tracking**: Tracking talking, his tape-slot mouth wide open; Degauss's mouth closed.
3. **critics-degauss**: Degauss talking, the pixel mouth on his screen a wide round "oh";
   Tracking's mouth closed.

## Cue: two images

4. **cue-rest** and 5. **cue-sing**. The teleprompter. **No fur, no hood of fur**: the angled glass
hood is bare metal and glass with scuffs and a little tape. Its face on the glass: two words circled
in pencil for eyes, "dear" and "friend", and a pencil-line smile; in "sing" the pencil mouth is a
clearly open round "o" and the circled words lift a little like raised brows. Keep the monitor chest,
red tally light, scroll knob, charcoal felt arms, the chrome mic-stand leg and the wooden chair leg,
a black cable. Standing, one hand raised, a head shorter than the others. Same pose in both.
