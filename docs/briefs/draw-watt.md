# Brief: Watt, the power strip (two images)

Make **two images with your image generation tool and nothing else**: run no commands, process no
images, write no files. List which is which at the end.

Read `docs/briefs/moppet-world.md` and `docs/briefs/studio-404-cast-art.md` first: same world, same
style block (square, flat chroma blue #0047BB, full body, three-quarter front view, centred, feet
near the bottom, no floor, nothing floating), and the rules for what a Moppet is. The attached
image is the old Watt: keep the power strip and the rainbow cable tail, **drop the felt squirrel
head**.

**Watt** keeps everyone switched on: every Moppet plugs into him. He's a cheerful, busy little
helper, warm and quick, happiest when every socket is full.

- **Body:** an old cream-white power strip standing upright, a little scuffed, with a row of
  sockets down his front; two of them have other Moppets' plugs in them, their cables trailing
  off-frame.
- **Face:** the top socket is his face: its two slots are his eyes, slightly tilted like a smile in
  the eyes, and the round earth hole below is his mouth, outlined in soft felt so it can open.
- **Switch:** a red rocker switch near his face, glowing softly.
- **Tail:** a big bushy tail of tangled rainbow cables curling up behind him.
- **His own plug** dangles from his base on a short cord, unplugged.
- Felt arms in warm grey with four-fingered hands, one arm raised in a wave; short felt legs with
  little felt shoes.

Two images, same pose and framing:
1. **watt-rest**: mouth closed, a happy face.
2. **watt-sing**: the round mouth wide open as if singing, the switch glowing brighter.
